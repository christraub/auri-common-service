Privacy policy

Auri Common Service · effective 29 October 2026

Who uses it. Auri Common Service is a private file-synchronisation utility used only by its owner, Chris Traub. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses. With the owner's permission, it reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive, solely to copy files between the owner's computer and that Drive and to keep a log of those copies.

What it does not do. It does not collect data from anyone else, does not share, sell or transfer Google user data to any third party, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage. Copied files stay in the owner's own computer and Google Drive. The access token is stored only on the owner's computer.

Revoking access. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
